Exercise Library

Exercise
Barbell Squat
A compound lower body exercise. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, bar resting on upper back. Bend knees and lower hips until thighs are parallel to the floor, then drive back up.
Push-up
A bodyweight upper body exercise targeting chest, shoulders, and triceps. Keep core tight and lower chest to the floor, then push back up to starting position.
Plank
A core stability exercise. Hold a straight body position on forearms and toes, keeping hips level. Focus on breathing and bracing the core throughout.
Romanian Deadlift
A hip hinge movement targeting hamstrings and glutes. Hold the bar at hip level, push hips back while lowering the bar along the legs, keeping a neutral spine.
Dumbbell Shoulder Press
An overhead pressing movement targeting deltoids and triceps. Press dumbbells from shoulder height to full arm extension overhead, then lower with control.
Walking Lunge
A unilateral lower body exercise. Step forward into a lunge position, lower the back knee toward the floor, then bring the rear foot forward to continue walking.
Cat-Cow Stretch
A gentle spinal mobility exercise. On all fours, alternate between arching the back upward (cat) and letting it sag downward (cow), moving with the breath.
